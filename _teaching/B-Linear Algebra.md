Teaching assistant for large introductory linear algebra course taught by [Emily Riehl](https://emilyriehl.github.io/). We covered topics such as reduced row echelon form, linear systems of equations, abstract vector spaces and linear maps (basics, no canonical forms), determinants, eigenvalues, and inner product spaces. Otto Bretscher's "Linear Algebra with Applications" was the course text.
