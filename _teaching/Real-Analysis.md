Course assistant and grader for small upper-level real analysis course taught by [Richard Brown](https://mathematics.jhu.edu/directory/richard-brown/). We covered sequences, limits of functions, differentiation on the real line, integration (Darboux integral), and sequences of series of functions using Jiri Libl's "Basic Analysis: An Introduction to Real Analysis" as our text.
